# Piarco Live Brainstorm

T&T Flight Board should stay focused on passenger flight tracking for Trinidad and Tobago airports. The strongest direction is a public, data-dense airport board that combines flight status, route context, local weather, and traveler-facing arrival/departure signals for POS/TTPP and TAB/TTCP.

## Core Audience

- Travelers checking departure status.
- Family and friends meeting arriving passengers.
- Airport watchers who want a cleaner Trinidad-focused tracker.
- Airport-adjacent businesses such as taxis, hotels, lounges, and retail teams.

## Product Angles

### Public Flight Tracker

- Live-style map centered on Trinidad and Tobago.
- Arrivals and departures board for POS/TTPP and TAB/TTCP.
- Weather strip with wind, visibility, cloud, pressure, and active runway.
- Watchlist for flights that matter to the user.
- Mobile-first quick check for people on the move.

### Airport-Adjacent Business Dashboard

- Arrival wave summaries for staffing and pickup planning.
- Delay alerts for hotels, taxi dispatchers, and tour operators.
- Daily passenger movement pulse based on scheduled arrivals and departures.
- Embedded display mode for lobbies and dispatch desks.

### Traveler Notifications

- Flight watchlist with status changes.
- ETA updates for inbound flights.
- Delay and gate-change prompts when real schedule data is connected.
- Weather-aware warnings when local conditions may affect timing.

## Differentiators

- Trinidad and Tobago context from the first screen.
- Trinidad and Tobago-first defaults instead of global tracker clutter.
- Long-haul route context for London, Miami, New York, Houston, Toronto, Amsterdam, Panama City, and regional Caribbean routes.
- Fast board scanning for non-technical users.
- Operational visual language without looking like a generic travel app.

## Data Priorities

1. Scheduled arrivals and departures for POS and TAB.
2. Live or delayed aircraft position data when available.
3. METAR and weather interpretation for TTPP and TTCP.
4. Route geometry and airport metadata.
5. Historical delay patterns if the product grows.

## Monetization Ideas

- Sponsored public flight board.
- Airport-adjacent business subscriptions for alerts and daily summaries.
- White-label lobby screens for hotels, transport operators, and lounges.
- Premium historical reporting for recurring operational planning.

## Near-Term Validation

- Interview airport transfer operators.
- Interview hotels near Piarco and Port of Spain.
- Interview frequent travelers and people who regularly meet arriving passengers.
- Test whether a simple public tracker gains repeat usage during peak travel windows.
- Validate whether businesses will pay for alerts, summaries, or display mode.

## Positioning

> A Trinidad and Tobago flight tracker built around POS and TAB arrivals, departures, weather, and route context.
